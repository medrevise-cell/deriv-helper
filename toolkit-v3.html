🎓 Trade academy

1. How digit contracts work

You predict the last digit of the next tick. Even/Odd wins on half the digits. Over 2 wins on 3–9, Under 8 on 0–7. Matches wins on exactly one digit.

2. Payout vs break-even

The safer the contract, the smaller the payout. At roughly 19% profit on a win (Under 8), you must win about 84% of the time just to break even. Check the break-even rate before every setup.

3. Why digits are random

Synthetic indices use a random number generator. Streaks happen by chance, and a "due" digit does not exist. A signal score measures the recent past, not the next tick.

4. Risk rules

Risk about 1% of your balance per trade, set a daily loss limit and stop when it is hit. Never raise stakes to win back losses.

5. Test before you trade

Run Strategy pro on several markets, then trade a demo account for at least a week. If the results look like a coin flip, treat them that way.
